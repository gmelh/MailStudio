# Social icons

White icon shapes, 256px on transparency, that MailStudio colours when an email uses them
(see `server/mailstudio/social.py`): either on a coloured circle, or drawn in the colour.

They're made from **Font Awesome Free** icons by Fonticons, Inc., licensed under
[CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/) (https://fontawesome.com/license/free):
centred in a square and converted to PNG, otherwise unchanged. `website` is Font Awesome's
globe; the rest are its brand icons. The brands are trademarks of their owners, used here only
to link to the sender's own profiles.

To rebuild or add one, see `tools/build_social_icons.py`.
